# Emoji Stickers (ﾉ◕ヮ◕)ﾉ*:・ﾟ✧

**react to anything, in one drag.**

Over 1,500 emoji as big, crisp stickers in Microsoft's friendly Fluent style. Vote on sticky notes, cheer a teammate, or make a lesson pop ♡

![Searching Emoji Stickers for star, party, wave and heart, picking a skin tone and the Flat style, and dragging each sticker onto a Drawdy board](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-emoji-stickers/main/docs/media/emoji-stickers.gif)

## let's stick ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **smiley** in the extension rail.
2. Search by meaning, like *party*, *love* or *lol*, or pick a group: Smileys, People, Animals & nature, Food & drink and more. Press `/` to jump to search.
3. Drag a sticker onto the board. Or click it to add it in the middle of your view; more clicks line up in a row.
4. Press **Enter** in the search box to add the top result.

> (・_・;) **stickers still loading?** They download as you scroll, so give a slow connection a second.

---

## make it yours (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

- **Shaded** or **Flat**: soft light and depth, or clean solid colours.
- **Skin tone**: the classic yellow or one of five tones, for every person and hand.
- **Size**: S, M, L or XL. L is the default, so stickers stand out.
- **Label**: add the emoji's name underneath, grouped so they move together.
