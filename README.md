# Emoji Stickers

Every emoji as a big, crisp sticker for your Drawdy board, in Microsoft's friendly Fluent style. React to an idea, mark a mood, or decorate a board in one drag.

## What it's for

- **Meetings and retros**: thumbs up, hearts, fire and party poppers to vote and react on sticky notes.
- **Classrooms**: animals, food, weather, sports and objects that make lessons and worksheets fun.
- **Any board that needs some life**: birthday plans, trip ideas, mood boards and games.

## How to use

1. Click the **Emoji Stickers** button on the right of the board.
2. Pick a group: **All**, **Smileys**, **People**, **Animals & nature**, **Food & drink**, **Travel & places**, **Activities**, **Objects** or **Symbols**. Or search by name or meaning, such as party, love or lol; press `/` to jump to the search box.
3. Drag an emoji onto the board and let go where you want it. Or click it to add it in the middle of your view; more clicks line emoji up in rows instead of stacking them.
4. In the search box, press Enter to add the top result.

## Options

- **Size**: S, M, L or XL, from 32 to 256 board units. L is the default, so stickers stand out.
- **Style**: **Shaded** has soft light and depth; **Flat** has clean, solid colours.
- **Skin tone**: the default yellow or one of five skin tones, for every person and hand that has them.
- **Label**: also add the emoji's name, centred under it and grouped with it so they move together.

## Tips

- Your last 12 emoji stay in a **Recent** row, with the skin tone you used, and the palette remembers your options.
- Stickers are vector images, so they stay sharp however far you zoom in.

## Privacy

- The emoji download from the Iconify API as you scroll. Nothing you draw or type is sent anywhere.
- Your recent emoji and options are saved in this browser, so they are the same on every board you open here.
